## Why

The author rejected the exact pixel F1 and the Hausdorff distance as visual measures (a 100-px line shifted by one pixel
scores F1 = 0; one far pixel sets the Hausdorff) and asked for methods to be compared by how much they compress while the
result still looks the same, at every scale, including polygons with thin parts.

## Library (`src`)

- `Core.Common\Mathematics\Statistics\RasterComparison.cs`: every drawn pixel of both rasters ranked by its distance to
  the other raster (exact EDT). New: `ExcessDisplacement` (mean (d − √2)² beyond the 8-neighbourhood),
  `Displacement95`, `FractionBeyond1/2/3`, `OmissionBeyond2` (original pixels farther than 2 px from the result),
  `CommissionBeyond2`. TSV: 15 columns (`EmptyTsv` = 14 tabs).
- `Core.MachineLearning\...\SyntheticData\OracleLabelledTriad.cs`: `MaxDisplacement`, `ExcessDisplacement`,
  `IsRetainedByDisplacement(d)`.
- `Core.Spatial\Analysis\Simplification\Simplifications.cs`: `SimplifyByRamerDouglasPeuckerSegment`,
  `SimplifyByBeforeOpeningWindowSegment` — RDP and BOPW with the distance to the chord segment (the published methods
  use the infinite line, the textbook definition, and are unchanged). Used only in E12.

## Research tool (`research\IRI.Maptor.Res.LRSimplification`)

- `OracleLabeller`: displacement per triad; rules `hd-1`, `hd-1.5`, `hd-2`; report of the displacement of the cells the
  published table removes. `LRHelper.OracleFile` = `oracle-triads-v3.json`; v1 and v2 are protected from overwriting.
- `Common\TopologyCounts.cs`: collapsed rings and self-intersections; logged in `VisualSimplificationLog`
  (`CollapsedRings`, `SelfIntersections`) for the record — topology is out of scope (rendering only).
- `SingleFeatureVaryingThresholdExperiment.Baseline`: optional `Filter` (point-list method outside the
  `SimplificationType` enum) and `Apply`; `RunVisualLimitAsync` (threshold sweep with optional early stop).
- `ResubmissionExperiments`: `DisplacementLabelsAsync` (E9), `CoarseScreensAsync` (E10), `VisualLimitAsync` (E11),
  `ThinPartsAsync` (E12); `LrsExperiment` entries `E9DisplacementLabels`, `E10CoarseScreens`, `E11VisualLimit`,
  `E12ThinParts`.
- `ExperimentStatistics`: the new columns in `Stats-by-layer/-wilcoxon/-matched.txt`.

## Tests (`tests\IRI.Maptor.Tests`)

- `Common\RasterComparisonTest.cs`: shifted line costs nothing, far spike is charged; five 3-px pixels outweigh one 4-px
  pixel; 95th percentile vs Hausdorff; omitted spike is omission, not commission.
- `Spatial\SegmentDistanceSimplificationTest.cs`: line-distance BOPW drops a thin arm, segment BOPW keeps it;
  line-distance RDP drops a spike beyond the chord end, segment RDP keeps it; both agree on a straight line.
- 133 tests pass (`MachineLearning`, `Simplification`, `RasterComparison`, `SegmentDistance`, `LiOpenshaw` filters).
