## Library (`src`)

- `Core.Spatial\...\Simplifications.cs`, `SimplifyRingAsClosedLine`: RDP (both variants) honours `Retain3Points` on rings. A
  ring divided at its farthest vertex can keep two vertices (long thin polygon) or one (tolerance larger than the ring);
  the vertex farthest from the first one, then the vertex farthest from the line through the two, are added. JSS and ISPRS
  ran with `Retain3Points = false` (unaffected).
- `Presentation.Wpf\Helpers\SimplificationHelper.CompareBySingleFeature`: optional `measureCollapsed` — a result of another
  geometry type is measured instead of skipped (the published drivers keep the skip); TLVD is NaN when the structures
  differ (it used to throw and stop the run).

## Research tool (`research\IRI.Maptor.Res.LRSimplification`)

- `SingleFeatureVaryingThresholdExperiment.MetricCanvas` and `RunVisualLimitAsync(..., marginPixels)`: one canvas pixel per
  zoom-level pixel and a 2-px margin (`ResubmissionExperiments.MetricMarginPixels`). `WebMercatorUtility.ToScreenSize`
  enlarges by 1.2 and truncates and the renderer fits the extent, so features used to be drawn at 1.0–1.2× their scale
  with the tighter side on the canvas edge (lines rounded off the canvas). In runs with the margin the logged `MaxExtent`
  is the canvas's larger side.
- `FeatureSelectionChecksExperiment` (`--run FeatureSelectionChecks`): all 127 feature subsets by CV and
  leave-one-base-length-out, bootstrap of the coefficient ranking, stability selection.
- `LabelChoiceExperiment` (`--run LabelChoiceValidation`): validation sample (published protocol, seed 2027, test features
  excluded) and LRSv4 on six label sets against RDP/RW/NOPW/BOPW.
- `ResubmissionExperiments`: `CombinedScalesAsync` (E13, replaces E7/E8/E10/E11; screens 256–16) with `CombinedScales512Async`
  and `CombinedScales16Async`; `ThinPartsAsync` / `ThinParts512Async` on `DataWm\ThinParts-2026-10-06` (split protocol);
  E5 and E6 use LRSv4.
- `LRHelper.LrsExperiment`: `FeatureSelectionChecks`, `LabelChoiceValidation`, `E13CombinedScales`, `E13CombinedScales512`,
  `E13CombinedScales16`, `E12ThinParts512`.
